Path to Dominance

Four stages of conquest

I

Select Your Faction

Five factions offer distinct strategic advantages. The Empire prioritizes technology. The Federation controls resources. The Alliance specializes in defense. The Syndicate dominates trade. The Legion lives for combat. Choose according to your doctrine.

II

Claim Territory

Neutral zones await conquest. Rival commanders hold key positions. Expand methodically or strike aggressively. Balance offense with defensive preparation as your borders grow.

III

Build Your Engine

Develop production capacity, establish supply networks, fund research programs. Economic strength translates directly to military power. A commander who cannot field an army cannot hold territory.

IV

Execute Total War

Deploy combined arms, outmaneuver opponents, secure objectives. Form coalitions or fight solo. Only one commander claims final victory. The world bends to whoever holds the map.
